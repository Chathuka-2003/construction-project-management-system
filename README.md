# EcoBuild Construction Management System

## 📌 Project Overview

EcoBuild Construction Management System is a digital platform designed to automate and manage construction material supply operations.
The system replaces manual processes such as order handling, appointment scheduling, inventory tracking, vehicle dispatching, and customer record management with a centralized digital solution.

This project improves efficiency, transparency, and decision-making for construction-related businesses in Sri Lanka.

## ✨ Key Features

### 👥 Customer Management
- Customer registration and login system
- Online order placement functionality
- Appointment request submission
- Real-time order and appointment status tracking

### 📦 Order & Appointment Management
- Streamlined order creation and approval workflow
- Appointment issuing with comprehensive status updates
  - Pending
  - Approved  
  - Issued
- Automatic approval time recording for tracking

### 📊 Inventory Management
- Digital stock records for accurate tracking
- Real-time material quantity monitoring
- Daily sand and gravel loading reports

### 🚛 Vehicle & Transport Management
- Efficient vehicle allocation system
- Delivery scheduling optimization
- Real-time dispatch status tracking

### 👨‍💼 Staff Management
- Multi-role user system
  - Admin
  - Staff
  - Customer
- Staff attendance tracking
- Enhanced internal communication tools

### 💰 Payment & Reports
- Comprehensive payment record management
- Invoice handling and processing
- Automated reporting system
  - Daily reports
  - Weekly reports
  - Monthly reports
 
## 🚀 Future Enhancements
